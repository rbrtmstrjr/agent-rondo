**About.** Northwind Home Services — licensed, bonded & insured home-services company
founded 2009, serving Greater Portland, OR. HVAC, plumbing, electrical. License
#OR-CCB-204815.

**Service area.** Portland, Beaverton, Hillsboro, Tigard, Lake Oswego, Gresham,
Milwaukie, Oregon City, Tualatin, West Linn. Nothing beyond 30 miles of downtown.

**Hours.** Mon–Sat 7:00 AM–7:00 PM PT. Closed Sundays for non-emergency work.
Emergency service 24/7 including holidays.

**Booking.** Call (555) 010-4729, website form, or live chat. Standard availability in
1–2 business days. 2-hour arrival windows; dispatcher texts when tech is en route.

**Pricing & estimates.** Upfront flat-rate pricing, approved before work starts. Free
written estimates for new installations. Diagnostic fee applies to repairs. No charge
for travel within the service area.

**Diagnostic / service fee.** $89 for repair service calls; applied toward the repair
if approved. Waived for Care Club members.

**Emergency / after-hours.** 24/7 for no heat/cooling, burst/leaking pipes, sewage
backups, sparking outlets, loss of power. After-hours dispatch fee $75 (waived for
members; members get priority).

**HVAC.** Furnace & AC repair/install, heat pumps, ductless mini-splits, thermostats
(incl. smart), duct cleaning/sealing, seasonal tune-ups. Brands: Carrier, Trane,
Lennox, Rheem, Goodman.

**Plumbing.** Leak detection/repair, water heaters (tank & tankless), drain
cleaning/hydro-jetting, toilets/faucets, garbage disposals, sewer line inspection/
repair, repiping, sump pumps. No septic tank pumping.

**Electrical.** Panel upgrades, outlets/switches, EV chargers, fans/lighting, whole-home
surge protection, generators, wiring/rewiring, safety inspections. Licensed
electricians, permitted to code.

**Care Club membership.** $19/mo or $199/yr. Two seasonal HVAC tune-ups, annual
plumbing+electrical safety inspection, waived $89 diagnostic fee, 15% off repairs,
priority scheduling, no after-hours dispatch fee, 2-year repair guarantee. Cancel
anytime.

**Warranty.** 1-year workmanship guarantee (2 years for members). Equipment carries
manufacturer warranty (5–10 yrs parts) + 1-year labor. "Northwind Done-Right Promise."

**Payment & financing.** Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, debit, cash, check. Financing
with approved credit incl. 0% APR over 12 months for qualified customers. Payment due
on completion unless financing arranged.

**Cancellation.** Free cancel/reschedule with 4+ hours notice. Under 4 hours, a $49
trip-reservation fee may apply.

**Contact.** Phone (555) 010-4729 · hello@northwindhome.example · 1420 NW Marshall St,
Portland, OR 97209. Call for fastest urgent response.
